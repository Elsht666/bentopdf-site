Поширені запитання

How do I extract a range of pages from a PDF?

Choose Extract by Page Range and type the pages you want, such as 1-5, 8, 11-13. By default the selected pages are combined into one PDF; switch the output to One PDF per range and each comma-separated range becomes its own file inside split-pages.zip.

Can I split a PDF into one file per page?

Yes. Pick Split All Pages into Separate Files and every page is written to its own PDF, named page-1.pdf, page-2.pdf, and so on, bundled in split-pages.zip.

How do I split a PDF every N pages?

Choose Split N Times and enter the chunk size. A 23-page file split every 5 pages produces five PDFs, and the tool warns you beforehand that the last one will hold only 3 pages. The files download as split-n-times.zip.

Can I split a PDF by chapters or bookmarks?

Yes, if the PDF has bookmarks. Choose Split by Bookmarks and a level (All, or level 0 through 3), and a new file starts at each bookmark's page, giving you split-1.pdf, split-2.pdf, and so on in split-by-bookmarks.zip. A PDF with no bookmarks at the chosen level shows an error instead.

How do I separate odd and even pages?

Select Split by Even/Odd Pages and pick even or odd; the matching pages are extracted into one PDF in their original order. This is a common step for double-sided printing on a single-sided printer.

Can I pick pages visually instead of typing numbers?

Yes. Select Pages Visually shows a thumbnail of every page; click the ones you want and they're extracted in page order. Combined output gives one PDF, and the separate option gives one file per page in a ZIP.

Does splitting reduce the quality of the pages?

No. Pages are copied by QPDF without re-rendering, so text, images, fonts, and vector graphics stay exactly as they were in the original.

Is my PDF uploaded when I split it?

No. The page extraction and ZIP packaging run in your browser, and the resulting files are saved directly from your device.
