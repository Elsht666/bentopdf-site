To get a PDF under 200KB, add the file below, pick the Balanced or Aggressive level, and download the result. The compression happens locally in your browser. no upload, no signup, no file limit.

When 200KB is the target

A 200KB ceiling shows up on bank KYC uploads, scholarship and exam registration portals, and many national e-government services. It is a friendlier target than 100KB: most everyday documents reach it with the Balanced level and no visible quality loss.

Getting a stubborn file under 200KB

If a scanned document stays above 200KB, step up to Aggressive, which downscales images harder while keeping text crisp. Multi-page scans benefit from removing blank or irrelevant pages first. every scanned page you delete is often 100KB saved before compression even starts.

What quality should I expect at 200KB?

Typed documents look identical. Scans keep readable text at Aggressive level; photos inside the PDF will look slightly softer but remain clear on screen.

The portal wants under 200KB but my file is 10MB. possible?

Usually yes, if most of the weight is images. A 10MB scan at 300 DPI color typically compresses to 150 to 400KB at Aggressive level. If it lands just above 200KB, re-run with Extreme.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. Every BentoPDF tool is free with no account, and because the work happens in your browser there are no server costs to pass on to you.
