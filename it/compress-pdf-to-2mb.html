Avviso

To compress a PDF to 2MB, add your file below, choose the Light or Balanced level, and download the result. A 2MB budget lets you keep high image quality. ideal for portfolios and reports. and the compression runs entirely on your device.

Quality-first compression

With 2MB to work with, you rarely need destructive settings. The Light level removes dead weight. duplicate fonts, unused objects, editing leftovers. and keeps images nearly untouched, which suits design portfolios, photography-heavy reports, and print-ready documents that still have to pass an upload check.

Common 2MB situations

University thesis submission systems, conference paper uploads, and many procurement portals cap files at 2MB. Documents exported from Word or Google Docs almost always fit after Light compression; InDesign or Canva exports with full-resolution imagery may need Balanced.

Domande frequenti

Which level keeps the best image quality?

Light. It focuses on structural savings and gentle image optimization. Use Balanced only if Light leaves you above 2MB.

Why is my exported PDF so large in the first place?

Design tools often embed full-resolution images and complete font files. Compression subsets the fonts and re-encodes images at sensible resolution, which is where the large savings come from.

Can I check the result before downloading?

You see the compressed size immediately after processing, and you can re-run with a different level as many times as you like. nothing is uploaded, so iteration is instant.
